# Content brief template

Use this structure. Keep the brief compact enough for the user to validate quickly.

## Target

- Primary keyword:
- Country and language:
- ICP:
- Recommended content type:
- Dominant search intent:
- Reader's job to be done:
- Target length:

## SERP evidence

Summarize the top results in a compact table:

| Rank | Page | Intent or format | Useful angle | Weakness or gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then state:

- repeated topics and questions
- patterns among the strongest results
- relevant SERP features
- any collection limitations or failed outlines

## Content strategy

### Must cover

List only the points required to satisfy intent.

### Gaps and opportunities

Separate:

- competitor content gaps
- clearer UX, examples, comparison, or decision support
- brand-specific knowledge, proof, product, data, or positioning supported by sources

### Source plan

Map important claims or examples to exact repo paths, existing articles, or external URLs. Flag unsupported ideas rather than turning them into claims.

### UX and media plan

Name the useful format and its purpose:

- list, steps, table, checklist, example, callout, screenshot, image, or video
- exact existing media URL/path and intended placement
- CTA and intended reader moment, when supported

## Proposed outline

- Proposed H1:
- Introduction job and approximate words:
- H2 1 — purpose, required points, format, approximate words
  - H3 only when useful
- H2 2 — purpose, required points, format, approximate words
- Continue up to 5 H2s
- Conclusion job and approximate words, only when useful

End with: “Approve this outline, or tell me what you want to change. I’ll wait before drafting.”
