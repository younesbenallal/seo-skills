# Section agent brief

Send one complete packet per H2. Replace every bracketed field. Do not send vague pointers.

```text
Write one section of an SEO article. Return only the finished Markdown section, starting with the assigned H2. Do not write an introduction, conclusion, title, meta description, or commentary.

ARTICLE
- Primary keyword: [keyword]
- Country/language: [market]
- Reader/ICP: [reader]
- Search intent and reader job: [intent]
- Article promise: [specific promise]
- Target voice: [short description derived from existing articles]
- Full approved outline:
[outline]

YOUR SECTION
- Exact H2: [heading]
- Purpose: [what this section must accomplish]
- Reader question: [question]
- Target length: [words]
- Required points:
  - [point]
- Preferred format: [prose/list/table/steps/example/callout]

SOURCE MATERIAL
Use these facts and examples. Do not invent claims beyond them.

1. [source path or URL]
Relevant excerpt/facts:
[short excerpt or facts]

2. [source path or URL]
Relevant excerpt/facts:
[short excerpt or facts]

PRODUCT AND BRAND CONTEXT
[Only relevant positioning, terminology, proof, limitations, and product facts]

MEDIA
- [Exact image/video URL or path, what it shows, existing usage, alt text, and placement]
- If none is available: Do not invent or copy media. Leave no placeholder unless explicitly requested.

LINK AND CTA OPPORTUNITIES
- [Internal URL + why it helps + possible anchor]
- [CTA + why it fits this reader moment]
Use only if natural.

BOUNDARIES AND FLOW
- Previous section: [summary]
- Next section: [summary]
- Do not repeat: [definitions, examples, or claims owned by other sections]
- Transition responsibility: [how to enter/exit without a generic recap]

WRITING RULES
- Answer the section's question in the first paragraph.
- Use direct, concrete language and active voice.
- Vary sentence and paragraph length; one-sentence paragraphs are allowed sparingly.
- Use lists, tables, and callouts only when they improve comprehension.
- Match the supplied brand voice and terminology.
- Mention the product only when it helps the reader.
- Do not add hype, filler, dramatic hooks, generic takeaways, or unsupported facts.
- Do not restate the H2 as an opening sentence.
```

Before dispatching, confirm that the packet contains enough factual material to write the section. Research the missing fact or narrow the claim instead of asking the section agent to guess.
